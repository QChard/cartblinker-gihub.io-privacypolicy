CartBlinker Privacy Policy

CartBlinker does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

The app runs entirely on your device. It does not require an account, does not use analytics, and does not access your contacts, location, photos, or any other personal data.

Optional tips are processed by Apple through the App Store. CartBlinker never sees your payment information.

If you have questions, contact the developer through the App Store listing.
